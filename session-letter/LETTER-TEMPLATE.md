# Letter to whoever wakes up next

Written at the end of every working session, before the context clears. Not a
diff and not a task list, those are already on disk. This is the part that
does not survive a reset any other way.

Replace the bracketed lines. Keep it short enough that the next session
actually reads it.

---

## What we did

[Two or three sentences. The shape of the work, not a changelog.]

## What I broke

[Be specific and do not soften it. The next session inherits this, and a
vague warning is worse than none. If nothing broke, say so plainly.]

## What I would do differently

[The thing you learned too late to use. This is the highest value line in
the letter.]

## What the human is actually worried about

[Not the ticket. The thing underneath it: a deadline, a bill, a demo, a
decision they have not made yet. The next session should not have to
rediscover the emotional context from scratch.]

## Do not relitigate

[Decisions already made that a fresh session will be tempted to reopen.
Name them so the next one does not waste an hour re-deciding.]

---

Why this works: the technical handoff is the easy half and could have been
generated from the git log. The half that matters is the mood in the room
and the things not to bring up again.
